# ISW50 coupon: what to check before you buy (2026)

Coupons change. Promotions stack differently by region. So the goal of this page is not to “promise a discount”, but to show you **how to verify** it in 60 seconds.

<h2 id="how">How it works</h2>

The ISW50 coupon is commonly presented as “€50 off orders from €500” for eligible carts. In practice:

- the cart must usually reach a **minimum**,
- some items may be excluded,
- an existing promotion can override the coupon.

So the only reliable check is the checkout summary right before payment.

<h2 id="apply">How to apply ISW50</h2>

1. Add your scooter (and optional accessories) to the cart.
2. Go to checkout.
3. Paste <code>ISW50</code> into the coupon field.
4. Apply it and verify the new total.
5. Only then confirm payment.

If the discount is not visible in the final recap, assume it’s not applied.

<h2 id="eligibility">Eligibility checklist</h2>

- My cart is above the required threshold.
- I’m buying an eligible product (some promos exclude specific models/bundles).
- No conflicting promotion is already applied.
- The discount is visible in the final checkout recap.

<h2 id="stacking">Stacking tips (simple and safe)</h2>

- If a sale is active, try two versions: with coupon vs without coupon.
- Don’t chase “stacking hacks”: just pick the best final total.
- Consider adding a useful accessory only if you were going to buy it anyway (helmet, lock, spare charger).
