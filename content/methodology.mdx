# How we test & review

This page explains how Cityscootlab evaluates scooters and deals, so you can understand our picks and how we update content.

## 1) What we evaluate (core criteria)

We focus on criteria that matter for **daily commuting**:

- **Range (realistic use):** marketing range is often optimistic; we compare claims with rider feedback and typical conditions.
- **Comfort:** tires, deck size, suspension, ride stability.
- **Safety:** braking setup, lights, traction, and overall build.
- **Portability:** weight, folded size, carry friendliness.
- **Practicality:** charging time, parts availability, warranty clarity.

## 2) Deals & coupons (how we handle pricing)

Prices move with promotions and region. When we mention a deal:

- we treat it as **“price at publish time”** and encourage checking the live price,
- we make coupon eligibility explicit (minimum cart value, exclusions),
- we avoid presenting limited-time promos as permanent pricing.

## 3) Data checks

We cross-check product claims using:

- manufacturer pages and manuals,
- retailer pages (prices/stock),
- independent references and community feedback when available.

See our [sources page](/sources) for the types of references we rely on.

## 4) Affiliate & sponsorship policy

Cityscootlab may earn commissions from affiliate links. We aim to keep recommendations **useful first** and clearly label sponsored/affiliate links.

## 5) Updates

Key pages are updated when we detect material changes (spec updates, new model releases, major price changes, coupon changes, or broken links).
