# Sources

Cityscootlab aims to publish clear, practical information. Here are the main types of sources we rely on.

## 1) Manufacturer information

- Official product pages (specs, versions, included accessories)
- Manuals / user guides (maintenance, safety, charging)
- Warranty and support documentation

## 2) Retailer data

- Live pricing and promo eligibility (when a coupon applies)
- Stock status and shipping constraints (region-dependent)

## 3) Safety & regulation references

Rules differ by country/city. We encourage checking local regulations (speed limits, where riding is allowed, required equipment).

## 4) Community feedback (when relevant)

When available, we use structured feedback (common issues, recurring maintenance tips) to sanity-check claims and highlight pitfalls.

## 5) Corrections

If you think we missed something or got something wrong, contact us with the page URL and the correction details: [Contact](/contact).
