# About Cityscootlab

Cityscootlab publishes practical picks, deals highlights, and guides about **isinwheel** e-scooters and accessories.

## Our goal

Help you choose a scooter that fits your daily commute (range, comfort, portability) and avoid common mistakes (wrong tires, weak braking, unrealistic range claims).

## Editorial independence & affiliate links

Some links on Cityscootlab are **affiliate links**. If you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This supports the site and helps us keep guides and updates free.

Affiliate relationships do **not** guarantee coverage and do **not** change our recommendations. When something is uncertain (availability, live price, spec variations), we say so and encourage you to verify on the retailer’s website.

## Updates & corrections

Prices, promos, and stock can change quickly. We update key pages when we spot meaningful changes. If you notice an error, please contact us and include the URL + a short explanation.

For corrections, feedback, or questions, reach us via our [contact page](/contact).
